Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding setup, permissions, and synchronization.

How fast do Roblox verification updates sync?

Roblox profile connections verify instantly via our custom code verification protocol. Once connected, rank mappings synchronize automatically in real-time or when members execute /verify update or /update in your server.

Why isn't my custom autoresponder trigger firing?

Check if the command has "Ignore Bots & Staff" toggled on, or if you have configured allowed/ignored role filters that exclude your profile. Also ensure Nora has "Send Messages" and "Embed Links" permissions in the specific channel.

Can I delete all my stored community data?

Yes. Under our absolute privacy commitments, users can execute the /mycard command in Discord at any time and click the "Erase Data" button to instantly and permanently wipe their warnings, leveling stats, and verification mappings.

How does the Top.gg Multi-Bot Tracker work?

You can connect your own Top.gg bot webhook URL and authorization token in your server settings. Nora receives vote webhooks from Top.gg, calculates streaks and multipliers, and immediately grants custom roles and coins to your voters.
