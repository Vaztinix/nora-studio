Nora Studio Terms of Service

Last Updated: September 2026 Standard: Human-Readable & Transparent Scope: Discord Bot, Web Dashboard & APIs

Welcome to Nora Studio. I created Nora to provide a powerful, privacy-first Discord bot for community automation, Roblox verification, leveling progression, and moderation. These Terms of Service ("Terms") explain the rules and guidelines for using the Nora Discord bot, our web dashboard at vaztinix.dev, and our verification tools.

By inviting Nora to your Discord server, logging into the dashboard, or running commands, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree with any part of them, please don't use Nora or simply remove the bot from your server.

1. Who Can Use Nora (Eligibility)

To use Nora and its services:

  • You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum legal age for digital services in your country), in direct compliance with Discord's Terms of Service and global online safety laws.
  • To add or configure Nora in a Discord server, you must have the appropriate Discord permissions (such as Manage Server or Administrator).
  • You agree to follow Discord's official Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.

2. Acceptable Use Policy (What's Allowed & What Isn't)

Nora is built to help positive, active communities grow and stay secure. To protect our servers and all users, the following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

  1. No Exploits or Bot Abuse: Do not attempt to spam, flood, crash, reverse-engineer, or execute denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks against Nora's API, database, or WebSocket gateways.
  2. No Harmful or Illegal Communities: Nora may not be used in servers that promote child exploitation (CSAM), malware distribution, hate groups, violent extremism, mass harassment, or unauthorized personal doxxing.
  3. No Data Scraping: You may not use automated scripts to harvest user IDs, server member lists, or data through Nora for commercial advertising or spam campaigns.
  4. No Impersonation: Do not claim to be an official developer or staff member of Nora Studio unless officially verified.
Zero-Tolerance for Abuse: If a server or user maliciously abuses Nora, exploits bot bugs, or violates these rules, I reserve the right to blacklist the server and user from all Nora services immediately without warning.

3. Server Management & Admin Responsibilities

As a server owner or manager configuring Nora, you hold the keys to how features behave in your community:

  • Role Hierarchy: Discord requires Nora's bot role to be placed above the roles it needs to assign or manage. You are responsible for properly arranging your server's role tree. Nora cannot modify roles that are above its own role.
  • AutoMod & Security Rules: While Nora's AutoMod and anti-raid filters are designed to be fast and accurate, server staff are responsible for choosing sensitivity thresholds that fit their server culture and reviewing flagged moderation cases.
  • Channel Permissions: Ensure your private mod-logs, ticket transcripts, and audit channels are locked to authorized moderators and admins only.

4. Roblox Verification & Group Rank Sync

Nora includes an integrated Roblox verification system and bi-directional group rank synchronization. Here is how we keep it 100% safe:

  • No Passwords or Session Cookies Ever: Nora will never ask for, touch, or store your Roblox password or your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie. All linking happens via public code verification or official Roblox Open Cloud OAuth2.
  • Public API Queries: Nora only checks public data (such as your Roblox user ID, username, and group rank number) to assign matching Discord roles.
  • Account Ownership: You are responsible for linking your own legitimate Roblox account. You can unlink your account at any time using /verify unlink or via the web portal.

5. Premium Subscriptions & Billing

Nora offers optional premium upgrades ("Nora Studio Plus") to unlock higher command limits, faster Roblox sync intervals, and expanded logging history.

  • Official Discord Monetization: All payments and recurring subscriptions are handled securely directly by Discord via Discord App Subscriptions. I do not see, store, or process your credit card numbers or banking information.
  • Managing Subscriptions: You can manage, upgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time in your Discord app under User Settings → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: Because all transactions are processed through Discord, all refund requests and billing questions are handled directly according to Discord's Refund Policy.

6. Availability, Maintenance & Disclaimers

I actively monitor and maintain Nora to provide maximum reliability, low latency, and 99.9%+ uptime. However, because Nora relies on third-party infrastructure (such as the Discord Gateway API and Cloudflare), services are provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis. I cannot guarantee that service will never experience brief maintenance windows or upstream Discord API hiccups.

7. Changes to These Terms

As Nora continues to grow and introduce new features, I may occasionally update these Terms. Any notable changes will be posted in our official Discord community server and reflected right here with an updated revision date. Continuing to use Nora means you accept the updated terms.

Nora Studio Privacy Policy

Policy: Privacy-First & Zero Data Selling Storage: Encrypted & Self-Service Purgeable Last Reviewed: September 2026

At Nora Studio, your privacy is taken seriously. I built Nora to respect user data: we only collect what is strictly required to make the bot's features work, and we never commercialize, sell, or profile your activity.

Our Zero-Selling Promise: Nora Studio does NOT sell, rent, or trade your data, server member lists, or telemetry to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Ever.

1. What Data We Collect and Why

Here is a complete, honest breakdown of every piece of data Nora stores and why it's needed:

Data Type What We Store Why It's Needed How Long It's Kept
Server Settings Discord Server ID, Channel IDs, Role IDs, Feature Toggles To save your server's custom configurations (welcome channels, automod rules, logging channels). Until Nora is removed from the server or data is purged by the server owner.
Roblox Verification Discord User ID mapped to Roblox User ID & Username To automatically give members the correct Discord roles based on their Roblox group rank. Until the user runs /verify unlink or deletes their account data.
Leveling & XP User ID, XP points, Level number, Rank card background choice To calculate server leveling progression, render rank cards, and assign level reward roles. Stored per server; can be wiped anytime via /mycard.
Moderation Logs Case ID, Target User ID, Moderator ID, Reason, Timestamp To provide server moderators with a record of past warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans. Stored per server for mod history; purgeable on request.
Ticket Transcripts Chat messages inside closed support ticket channels Saved so server staff can review closed customer support cases. Encrypted and automatically deleted after 30 days.
Rate Limiting (Security) Temporary anonymized IP hash in memory To protect login endpoints and web forms against malicious bot attacks. Cleared automatically from memory every 15–60 minutes. Never saved to a database.

2. What We NEVER Collect or Store

To avoid any ambiguity, Nora does NOT collect or have access to:

  • Your Discord password or payment credentials (all handled by Discord).
  • Your Roblox password or .ROBLOSECURITY cookies.
  • Your personal real-world name, home address, phone number, or government identity.
  • Your private direct messages (DMs) or server chat messages (outside of configured moderation logging and closed support tickets).

3. Data Security & Storage

All communication between your browser, Discord, and Nora's backend is encrypted with industry-standard TLS 1.3. Our databases are secured with encrypted volume storage, firewalls, and strict access controls to ensure your community data is protected from unauthorized access.

4. How to Delete Your Data (Self-Service)

You have full control over your data. You don't need to file support tickets or wait for an email to delete your records:

  • Individual User Wipe: Run /mycard in Discord, click the red "Erase Data" button, and confirm. Nora will instantly wipe your leveling XP, rank settings, and linked profiles.
  • Server Owner Wipe: Server owners can run /settings data-delete inside their server to immediately erase all server configs, leveling boards, and mod logs.
  • Roblox Unlink: Run /verify unlink in Discord or visit the Verification Portal to disconnect your Roblox account instantly.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Nora is not directed at children under the age of 13. In accordance with COPPA and Discord's Terms of Service, users under 13 are prohibited from creating Discord accounts or using Nora. If we discover that data belongs to an underage user, that data is permanently purged immediately.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, privacy concerns, or data requests, feel free to reach out directly:

  • Official Discord Server: Join our Nora Support Discord and open a private support ticket.
  • Direct Developer Contact: vaztinix@gmail.com or privacy@vaztinix.dev
