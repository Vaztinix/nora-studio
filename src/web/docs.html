Top.gg Vote Rewards & Integration Guide

Reward your community members for upvoting Nora and connected Discord bots on Top.gg with instant role assignments, customizable Leveling XP, weekend multipliers, and loyalty vote streaks.

Instant Webhook Delivery Pipeline

Votes cast on Top.gg are dispatched directly to Nora's dedicated API endpoint with sub-second latency. No manual claim commands required—rewards apply automatically the moment the vote is confirmed.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Step 1: Get Webhook Credentials from Nora Studio

Open the Nora Dashboard and navigate to the Top.gg Votes tab. Under 1. Connect on Top.gg, copy your unique Webhook URL and Webhook Authorization Secret.

Step 2: Connect Webhooks on Top.gg Developer Portal

Log in to top.gg → Open your Bot's Dashboard (e.g. top.gg/discord/bots/YOUR_BOT_ID/dashboard/integrations) → Go to Integrations & API → Scroll to Webhooks. Paste your Webhook URL and Authorization Secret into their respective fields, click Save, and then click Send Test to verify instant connectivity.

Step 3: Configure Voter Rewards & Role Hierarchy

In Nora Dashboard under 2. Voter Rewards & Roles, pick the Discord Voter Role to grant and set your base Leveling XP (default: 150 XP). Ensure the @Nora role is placed above the Voter reward role in Discord Server Settings.

Step 4: Announcements & 12-Hour Reminders

Select a dedicated Vote Announcement Channel for public celebration embeds and toggle 12-Hour Vote Reminders. Nora will automatically notify voters via DM when their 12-hour vote cooldown expires so they can vote again and keep their streak alive.

Important Discord Role Hierarchy Requirement

For Nora to assign voter roles successfully, ensure the @Nora role is positioned HIGHER than the Voter reward role in your Discord server under Server Settings → Roles. Discord blocks bots from assigning roles positioned above or equal to their highest role.

Reward Multipliers & Streak Mechanics

Nora calculates XP rewards dynamically based on voting frequency and weekend events:

Reward Factor Multiplier / Bonus Calculation Rules
Base XP Reward 150 XP Standard baseline XP granted for every verified upvote.
⭐ Weekend Multiplier 2x Multiplier Automatically active Friday 00:00 UTC through Sunday 23:59 UTC to match Top.gg weekend double votes.
🔥 Vote Streak Loyalty +25 XP per vote Increases by 1 for each consecutive vote cast within a rolling 36-hour window.
Formula (Base + Streak Bonus) × Multiplier Example: Base 150 + (3 streak × 25) = 225 XP × 2x Weekend = 450 Total XP!

Top.gg Slash Commands

Members and administrators can manage and view vote progress directly from Discord:

  • /vote — Generates the direct Top.gg voting link, shows the user's active streak, and displays current weekend multiplier status.
  • /setup topgg — Quick interactive setup panel in Discord to configure voter roles, rewards, and logging channels.

Automated Roblox Group Rank Sync

Seamlessly connect your Roblox group hierarchy to Discord server roles:

  • Group Binding: Use /roblox profile or configure your Group ID in the web dashboard under Onboarding & Safety.
  • Automated Role Mapping: Assign Discord roles directly matching Roblox group ranks (Guest, Member, Officer, Developer, Owner).
  • Zero-Friction Verification: Members verify using single-click bio description matching or cryptographic web handshake.

Roblox Group Anti-Impersonation

Nora caches group rank tokens securely and checks against the public Roblox Cloud API. Suspicious rapid rank manipulation triggers automated audit warnings in your logging channel.

Collaborative Math Counting

Keep channels active with sequential math counting:

  • Mathematical Parser: Members can submit numbers directly (100) or valid math expressions (50 + 50, 10^2, 25 * 4).
  • Strict Anti-Double-Count: A user cannot count twice in a row. Teamwork is mandatory.
  • Streak Records: Track highest server count streaks with /counting stats and top contributors with /counting leaderboard.
  • Configurable Strict Mode: Choose whether incorrect numbers reset the counter back to 0 or simply get deleted with a warning.

Native Discord AutoMod & Chat Defense

Protect server chat with sub-4ms native gateway filtering:

  • Phishing & Scam Link Blocker: Automatically detects and removes newly registered malicious domains and fake Nitro links.
  • Mass Mention & Raid Shield: Blocks messages containing >5 mentions and prevents mass spam joins.
  • Custom Mod Responses: Configure automatic timeout actions and tailored rule violation warnings via /setup automod.
